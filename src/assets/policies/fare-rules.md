# Fare Rules.pdf (Mock)

## Topics
- Basic Economy
- Flexible fares
- Fare upgrades
- Ticket changes
- No-show policy

## Guidance
Basic Economy fares often carry stricter change and upgrade limitations.
Flexible fares generally provide broader change options and lower penalties.
Upgrade eligibility depends on fare family, route, and seat inventory.
No-show policies can affect onward segments and residual ticket value.

## Example Questions
- Can Basic Economy be upgraded?
- What change fee applies to this fare?
- What happens if I miss the flight and no-show?

## Agent Response Pattern
1. Identify fare brand and ticket conditions.
2. Confirm applicable change and upgrade restrictions.
3. Explain penalties and alternatives clearly.
