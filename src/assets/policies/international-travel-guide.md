# International Travel Guide.pdf (Mock)

## Topics
- Passport validity
- Visa requirements
- Customs declarations
- Vaccination requirements
- International check-in windows
- Mandatory travel documents

## Guidance
Passengers traveling internationally are responsible for valid passports, visas, and required entry documentation.
Requirements vary by destination, nationality, and transit country.
Some routes require health documentation or vaccination proof.

## Example Questions
- Do I need a visa for my destination?
- How early should I check in for international travel?
- What documents are mandatory at boarding?

## Agent Response Pattern
1. Confirm itinerary, destination, and nationality.
2. Identify required travel and health documents.
3. Advise pre-travel verification through official sources.
