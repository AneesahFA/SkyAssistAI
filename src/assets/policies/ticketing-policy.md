# Ticketing Policy.pdf (Mock)

## Topics
- Name correction
- Date changes
- Seat changes
- Same-day standby
- Duplicate reservations

## Guidance
Minor name corrections may be allowed after identity verification.
Name transfers to another passenger are generally not permitted.
Date and seat changes depend on fare conditions and inventory.
Duplicate reservations should be reviewed promptly to prevent cancellations.

## Example Questions
- I have a spelling mistake on my ticket name.
- Can I change my travel date today?
- Is same-day standby available?

## Agent Response Pattern
1. Verify ticket ownership and fare conditions.
2. Apply permitted correction or change rules.
3. Explain fees, restrictions, and alternatives.
