# Pet Travel Policy.pdf (Mock)

## Topics
- Cats
- Dogs
- Service animals
- Carrier size
- Breed restrictions
- Cabin pet eligibility

## Guidance
Small cats and dogs may travel in-cabin when they remain inside an airline-approved carrier that fits beneath the seat.
Service animals are accepted according to current accessibility regulations and airline policy.
Fees, destination restrictions, and documentation requirements may vary by route.

## Example Questions
- Can my dog travel in cabin?
- What carrier dimensions are allowed?
- Are service animals accepted?

## Agent Response Pattern
1. Confirm route, species, and carrier dimensions.
2. Verify policy restrictions and required documentation.
3. Communicate fees and airport check-in steps.
