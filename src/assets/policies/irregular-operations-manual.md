# Irregular Operations Manual.pdf (Mock)

## Topics
- Weather delays
- Diversions
- Missed connections
- Rebooking
- Hotel vouchers
- Meal vouchers

## Guidance
During irregular operations, agents should rebook customers on the next viable itinerary.
Accommodation support such as meal or hotel vouchers may be available based on disruption cause, delay duration, and policy.
Communication should include rebooking status, expected wait times, and next action steps.

## Example Questions
- My flight was delayed. What can you do?
- I missed my connection due to weather.
- Do I qualify for a hotel voucher?

## Agent Response Pattern
1. Confirm disruption cause and elapsed delay.
2. Rebook first, then evaluate accommodation eligibility.
3. Provide clear follow-up instructions and escalation options.
