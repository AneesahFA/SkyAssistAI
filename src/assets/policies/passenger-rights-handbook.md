# Passenger Rights Handbook.pdf (Mock)

## Topics
- Compensation eligibility
- Denied boarding rights
- Delay and cancellation protections
- Accessibility rights
- Complaint channels

## Guidance
Passenger rights differ by country and governing regulations.
Eligible customers may qualify for compensation in involuntary denied boarding and specific delay/cancellation cases.
Accessibility rights and formal complaint channels should be provided clearly when requested.

## Example Questions
- Am I eligible for compensation?
- I was denied boarding. What are my rights?
- Where can I file a formal complaint?

## Agent Response Pattern
1. Identify governing jurisdiction and disruption type.
2. Assess eligibility using rights criteria.
3. Provide compensation path or complaint escalation steps.
