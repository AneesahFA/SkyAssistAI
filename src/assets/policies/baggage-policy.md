# Baggage Policy.pdf (Mock)

## Topics
- Carry-on baggage
- Checked baggage
- Oversized baggage
- Overweight baggage
- Lost baggage
- Delayed baggage

## Guidance
Most passengers may bring one carry-on bag and one personal item.
Checked baggage allowances depend on fare type, destination, and cabin.
Oversized or overweight baggage can incur additional fees and may require operational approval.
If checked baggage is delayed or lost, passengers should file a report at the baggage service office before leaving the airport.

## Example Questions
- Can I bring two carry-on bags?
- How much luggage can I check?
- What happens if my baggage is delayed?
- What is considered oversized baggage?

## Agent Response Pattern
1. Confirm fare type and route.
2. Verify baggage allowance and applicable fees.
3. Provide immediate escalation or reporting steps for delayed/lost baggage.
