# Supervisor Escalation SOP.pdf (Mock)

## Topics
- Unmatched policy inquiries
- Exception requests
- Time-critical disruptions
- Complaint escalation

## Guidance
If a policy answer cannot be confirmed, escalate to a supervisor with complete case context.
Include route, fare type, customer request, and any prior handling notes.
When needed, consult official policy repositories before finalizing customer commitments.

## Escalation Checklist
1. Capture customer name, booking reference, and itinerary.
2. Document exact policy question and attempted resolution.
3. Mark urgency level and required response timeframe.
4. Confirm handoff to supervisor or duty manager.
