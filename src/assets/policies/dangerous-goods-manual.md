# Dangerous Goods Manual.pdf (Mock)

## Topics
- Lithium batteries
- Power banks
- Firearms
- Alcohol restrictions
- Dry ice
- Camping fuel
- Electronic cigarettes

## Guidance
Spare lithium batteries and power banks should be carried in carry-on baggage only.
Damaged batteries and certain hazardous materials may not be transported.
Declared items such as firearms, approved alcohol quantities, or dry ice must comply with route-specific limits and safety rules.

## Example Questions
- Can I carry a power bank in checked baggage?
- Are e-cigarettes allowed in cabin bags?
- Can I travel with dry ice or camping fuel?

## Agent Response Pattern
1. Identify exact item and quantity.
2. Confirm if carry-on only, checked-only, restricted, or prohibited.
3. Provide declaration and safety packaging requirements.
