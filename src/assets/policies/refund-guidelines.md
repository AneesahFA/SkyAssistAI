# Refund Guidelines.pdf (Mock)

## Topics
- Refund eligibility
- Non-refundable tickets
- Flight cancellations
- Schedule changes
- Duplicate ticket refunds
- Processing timeline

## Guidance
Refund eligibility depends on fare rules and disruption type.
Refundable tickets are generally eligible for full refund.
Non-refundable tickets may qualify when the airline cancels a flight or makes a significant schedule change.
Approved refunds are commonly processed within 7 to 20 business days depending on payment method.

## Example Questions
- Can I get a refund for this ticket?
- My flight was canceled. What are my refund options?
- How long will the refund take?

## Agent Response Pattern
1. Confirm fare conditions and reason for refund.
2. Validate whether policy exception applies.
3. Communicate expected processing timeline by payment type.
